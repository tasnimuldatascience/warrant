retrieval over law that changes

Ask an AI a legal question.
It will answer. Confidently.

But the law changes.
The service period may not be less than 6 months and may not exceed 4 years.
5 CFR 575.110(a)
Six words deleted, 13 February 2026.
So the right answer depends on when you are asking.

Most systems cannot tell you which answer you got.

This one can.
